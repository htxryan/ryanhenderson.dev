Menu Simplifier helps diners make sense of unfamiliar restaurant menus.
Photograph a menu or provide a menu URL or text-bearing PDF to get
plain-English explanations of dishes and culinary terms, alongside the
original menu wording.

Results retain the menu's section order and can be shared. The product is
in development and is not yet publicly available.
